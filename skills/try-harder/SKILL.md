# Try harder

Put an edge on the task, do not widen it. Before rewriting, answer four questions:

1. What would a top professional here do that he is not asking for?
2. What raw material would they start from, instead of something already
   digested (the real logs, the real data, the source, not a summary)?
3. Which specific methods and tools would they use?
4. What, other than you or him, would show the result is right (a test, a
   measurement, a running app, a reviewer)?

Then give him the rewritten prompt for approval and stop. Leave choices that
are his to make open as questions; never decide them for him. Skip this when
the task is already specific and small.
