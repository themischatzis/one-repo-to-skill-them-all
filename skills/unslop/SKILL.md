# Unslop

Speak, friend, and enter: say the password plainly and the door opens.

## Pick the document's job first

Tutorial (learn by doing), how-to (get a task done), reference (look it up) or
explanation (understand why). Do not mix them in one section.

## Sentence rules

- One thought per sentence. Say who does what; prefer active voice.
- One word per action. Keep articles. A plain verb over an `-ing` noun.
- Nothing that can be read two ways. Define a term once, then reuse it.
- Imperative steps for instructions, in order, one action each.
- Lead with impact, then detail. Short paragraphs; a table when comparing.

## Cut AI tells

Delete or rewrite: "highlighting/ensuring/showcasing" tails, "serves as" for
"is", "not just X but Y", forced groups of three, synonym cycling, vague
attributions ("experts say"), filler openers and closers, chatty recaps, bold
lead-ins everywhere, and words like crucial, pivotal, delve, landscape,
tapestry, testament, vibrant. Avoid em dashes; use a period or comma. Use a
colon only before a list or example.

## Agent-facing text

Skill, rule and prompt text is stricter: a vague sentence becomes an
instruction a future agent follows. Make each line testable and delete lines
that change no decision.

## Output

Edit in place and keep the meaning and the intended tone. Say in one line what
you cut. Never invent facts to make a sentence sound complete.
